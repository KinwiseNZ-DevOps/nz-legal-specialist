Contents: guarantees on goods, guarantees on services, redress, contracting out.

Source: Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 (CGA), legislation.govt.nz. Applies when goods or services are acquired by a consumer (ordinarily for personal, domestic or household use), not business-to-business supply, unless the parties agree to contract out under the conditions in s 43(2). Verify current section numbers before citing.

## Guarantees in respect of supply of goods (Part 1, ss 5-15)

- **s 5** - guarantee as to title (the supplier has the right to sell the goods).
- **s 5A** - guarantee as to delivery (within a reasonable time, where no time is fixed).
- **s 6 / s 7** - guarantee as to acceptable quality: goods must be as acceptable as a reasonable consumer, fully informed, would regard as acceptable, considering nature, price, representations and all other relevant circumstances (fitness for common purposes, appearance, freedom from defects, safety, durability).
- **s 8** - guarantee as to fitness for a particular purpose the consumer made known.
- **s 9** - guarantee that goods comply with description.
- **s 10** - guarantee that goods comply with sample.
- **s 11** - guarantee as to price (a reasonable price, where no price was agreed).
- **s 12** - guarantee as to repairs and availability of spare parts within a reasonable time.
- **s 13** - express guarantees given by the supplier or manufacturer must be honoured.

## Right of redress against suppliers (Part 2, ss 16-24)

- **s 18** - the consumer's options where goods fail a guarantee: require the supplier to remedy the failure within a reasonable time, or, for a failure of substantial character (**s 21**) or one the supplier cannot or will not fix, reject the goods (**s 22**) and choose a refund or replacement (**s 23**), or keep the goods and claim damages for the drop in value.
- **s 20** - the right to reject goods is lost after a reasonable time, or if the goods are lost, destroyed or disposed of, or substantially altered.

## Right of redress against manufacturers (Part 3, ss 25-27)

- **s 25** - a consumer can claim directly against the manufacturer (not just the retailer) for breaches of the acceptable quality, description, express guarantee, repairs and spare parts guarantees.

## Supply of services (Part 4, ss 28-36)

- **s 28** - guarantee as to reasonable care and skill.
- **s 29** - guarantee as to fitness for a particular purpose made known to the supplier.
- **s 30** - guarantee as to completion within a reasonable time, where no time is fixed.
- **s 31** - guarantee as to a reasonable price, where no price was agreed or determinable.
- **s 32** - the consumer's options where services fail a guarantee: require remedy within a reasonable time, or, for a failure of substantial character, cancel the contract (subject to the cancellation provisions in **s 35** onward) or obtain damages.

## Contracting out (s 43)

- **s 43(1)** - a term that purports to exclude, restrict or modify the guarantees is void, except as s 43(2) allows.
- **s 43(2)** - if the goods or services are, or are to be, acquired for business purposes, the parties may agree in writing to contract out, provided it is fair and reasonable to do so.
- Drafting check: a "sold as is, no guarantees apply" clause aimed at a consumer transaction (personal, domestic or household use) is void under s 43(1); it only has a chance of working where s 43(2)'s business-purpose and writing conditions are genuinely met, and even then only if fair and reasonable. Flag accordingly and propose compliant wording (a repair-first remedy process, a liability cap, rather than a blanket exclusion).
