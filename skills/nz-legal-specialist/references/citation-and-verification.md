## Seed sources

Start here, not as a complete library but as a map to the primary source:

- [New Zealand Legislation](https://www.legislation.govt.nz) - the official, current text of every Act and regulation. This is the only source to quote a section from. Acts referenced by this skill set: Fair Trading Act 1986, Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, Commerce Act 1986, Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017, Privacy Act 2020, Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003, Constitution Act 1986, New Zealand Bill of Rights Act 1990.
- [Law Society Law Library databases](https://www.lawsociety.org.nz/professional-practice/law-library/databases/) - case law and secondary commentary, for practitioners with access.
- [Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/) - guidance on the Privacy Act 2020 and the Information Privacy Principles.
- [Consumer Protection (MBIE)](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/general-help/consumer-laws/fair-trading-act) - plain-language guidance on the Fair Trading Act 1986 and Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.
- [Commerce Commission](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/) - enforcement guidance on fair trading, unfair contract terms and competition law.
- [FinCap](https://fincap.org.nz/) - financial mentoring sector body; relevant to consumer credit terms and hardship clauses, not primary law.
- [Te Ara - The Encyclopedia of New Zealand](https://teara.govt.nz/en/te-ture-maori-and-legislation/print) - background on Māori and legislation, useful context for the Treaty of Waitangi, not a legal citation source on its own.

## How to verify a citation before using it

1. If the agent has live web access this session, open the section's page on legislation.govt.nz (or confirm it through a search) before writing the citation into a finding. Note the version date shown on the page ("Version as at ...").
2. Check the section has not been repealed, renumbered, or had a note added about pending amendments not yet in force.
3. Quote the section number exactly as the Act states it (e.g. "s 46L", not "s 46(L)" or "section 46-L").
4. If the point rests on how a court has applied the section, say so and flag that case law should be checked separately; this skill set does not hold a case law database.

## When web access is not available

- Prefix the citation: "s [number], [Act] (unverified this session - confirm on legislation.govt.nz)."
- Never drop the citation to avoid the caveat, and never state a section number you are not reasonably confident of; if genuinely unsure, name the Act and topic only, and say the exact section needs to be looked up.

## Citation format

Use: `s [number], [Act short title] [year]`, for example `s 9, Fair Trading Act 1986` or `s 22 (IPP 6), Privacy Act 2020`. For a subsection, add it: `s 46L(1)(a), Fair Trading Act 1986`. First use of an Act in a document spells out the full title; later uses may shorten to the common short title.
